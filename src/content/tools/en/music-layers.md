Before drawing a score, the director's intent is reduced to lanes per cut — volume, harmonic warmth and brightness, tension/release, each character's emotion, camera movement, reference tracks. In a video call, the director and I look at the same sheet.

It began as a spreadsheet and a pen; it is being moved into a program.
