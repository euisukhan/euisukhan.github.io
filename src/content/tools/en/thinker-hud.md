A tool for the moment context breaks while working. A single card in the corner of the screen shows **what I was doing / the next action / now and next on the schedule**, and captures spoken thoughts as candidates to review later.

The principle is *quiet by default, one line only when you drift* — information that is always on screen becomes its own distraction.
