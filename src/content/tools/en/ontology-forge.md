Summaries help you read faster. Mind maps show relations. Ontology Forge adds one thing on top: it makes visible **where I am saying more than my sources do.**

- Every claim can be traced back to the exact position in the original text.
- Colors mean the *kind* of sentence (observation, definition, value judgment, metaphor), not true/false.
- AI only proposes; nothing is stored until a human confirms it.

Currently in personal use. A repository link will appear when it is released.
